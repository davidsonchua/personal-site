Davidson Chua is the co-founder and CEO of Influencees, an AI-powered creator credibility and discovery platform for Southeast Asia, and the founder of Autosave, Singapore's most active automotive community with over 18,000 members. He holds a BSc (Honours) in Business Analytics from the National University of Singapore.
